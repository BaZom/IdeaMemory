# 1.1 — See your memories in the new design

Status: draft · Milestone: M1 Just me v1 · Security-relevant: yes (schema change) → review before merge

## Goal

Your existing memories show up in the oh! look, and you find any of them in seconds: by words, by kind, by who it’s for.

## Towards

- Builds **the memory model** of the [concept](../concept.md) (§1, §2) for the whole of M1, so 1.2–1.9 only add.
- Builds **My memory** and **a memory’s page** of the [target design](../design/oh-app.html), and the **oh! look** (colors, Nunito, paper notes, light and dark).
- Leaves out: saving and editing with the new fields (1.2), links filling in (1.4), plans (1.7), suggestions and the *A little nudge* / *Planned* tabs (1.7, 1.8), gifts and dates (1.9), spaces (M3), *Ask your memory* (M4).

## Design

Board: [1.1-memory-model-and-look.html](../design/slices/1.1-memory-model-and-look.html)

- **A Sign in**: the oh! wordmark, same fields as today.
- **B My memory**: header (logo, +, account); “My memory · 14 things, all yours.”; search; **All · Just me · With someone**; one row of kinds; paper notes, newest first, each with one hint (the most useful fact: an event date, who it’s for, the season, who told you, or its age).
- **C/D Search**: finds words in title, note, place, tags and names; “Nothing with ‘sushi’…” offers *Clear the search*.
- **E Empty**: “Your memory is empty, for now.” + *Remember my first thing*.
- **F A memory’s page**: kind, title, note, link, facts (season, weekends, cost, booking, place, event dates), who it’s for, who told you, tags. *Edit* and *Let it go* belong to 1.2 (they keep working as today until then).
- **G Error**: “I couldn’t reach your memory. Nothing is lost.” + *Try again*.
- **H Dark mode** follows the phone.
- **No bottom tabs** until 1.7: one page needs no navigation.

## Data

One forward migration on `ideas` (the database keeps that name; the app says *memory*):

| Column | Type and rule | From today | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `category` | `food, place, film, book, activity, trip, event, music, gift, other` or null | travel → trip, film-series → film, recipe → food, product → other | icons, kinds row |
| `note` | text ≤ 10 000 | `description`, plus `notes` appended after a blank line; `notes` is dropped, `description` renamed | page, search |
| `who_for` | text[] of first names, ≤ 10, each 1–40, no case-insensitive duplicates; **empty = just me** | empty | filter; M3 turns names into spaces |

| `weekend_only` | boolean, default false | false | 1.8 |
| `link_url` | text ≤ 2 000, `http(s)://` | null | page; 1.4 adds the preview |
| `event_start`, `event_end` | date; end ≥ start | null | hint; 1.8 |

- `sources` (the exact original text) stays as it is; 1.5 and 1.6 add the kinds `link` and `screenshot`.
- Later slices add: 1.4 link preview (`link_title`, `link_site`, `link_image`), 1.6 `needs_look`, 1.7 a `plans` table, 1.9 a `dates` table and `for_person`. Nothing in this slice has to move for them.
- Database checks mirror the API rules; owner-only row-level security and column grants stay as they are, extended to the new columns.

## API and app

- `GET /api/v1/ideas` gains `q` (all words must match title, note, location, told_by, tags or who_for; case-insensitive; wildcards escaped) and `who` (`me` = empty `who_for`, `others` = not empty), next to `category`. Pagination unchanged.
- Responses carry the new fields; create and update accept them (the 1.2 form uses them; until then the old form keeps working with `note`).
- Flutter: a theme (the target design’s color tokens light and dark, Nunito bundled as an asset, rounded paper shapes), the header, My memory (search with a short debounce, the who segment, the kinds row, paper notes), a memory’s page, the sign-in page. Kind icons: Material equivalents of the design’s icons.

## Security and privacy

Owner-only access as today, tested for the new columns (another user can’t read or filter by them). Search runs in the database over the caller’s own rows. No memory content in logs, including search words.

## Done when

1. You sign in on your phone and My memory looks like board B, light and dark.
2. Your old memories are all there, with their kinds mapped and their notes merged.
3. Searching “lena” or a place finds the right memories; *Just me / With someone* and the kinds row filter; nothing found says so.
4. A memory’s page shows everything it has; editing and deleting still work.
