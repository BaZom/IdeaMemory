Three personal concepts

No lists on the home screen. Each concept is one object you care about and one small ritual. The full collection is always one tap away, but it is not what you see first.

1. The someday jar

Like the jar of folded notes on a kitchen shelf. You drop ideas in as handwritten slips. When you have a free afternoon you pull one out, and the app tells you when you dropped it in. Pale glass blue, slips in the colors of their kind, Kalam handwriting, Young Serif greeting.

Free afternoon, Basem?

You pulled this one out.

Hike around the Schliersee, swim after if it’s warm
You dropped it in on 3 June. Saturday looks sunny.

36 more somedays inside

2. Dear future me

Every idea is a sealed letter to your future self, addressed to the moment it’s for: “To me, on a rainy Sunday.” The app hands you the right envelope at the right time; you open it or save it for later. Writing desk green, kraft envelopes, wax seals, handwritten addresses, typewriter notes.

Dear future me,

It’s Sunday and it’s raining. One of your letters is for exactly this.

M
sealed12 Oct
To me,
on a rainy Sunday

A film you wanted to see
on the biggest screen you can find.

3. Your sky

Every idea is a star in your own night sky. Kinds of ideas form constellations you name yourself (Hungry, Wander, Outside, Big screen); new wishes shine brightest and fade slowly so old ones get a nudge. Tap a star to see it. Deep ultramarine, warm gold, peach and mint stars, Gloock serif.

Your sky

42 wishes in 5 constellations
Brightest tonight
Long weekend in Lisbon
You wished for this two weeks ago: tram 28, pastéis de nata in Belém, a sunset at a miradouro.
