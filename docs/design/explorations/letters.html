Letters from your memory

Inspired by Sincerely’s evening letter ritual, made private: nobody else writes to you, your own memory does. Every evening it sends you three letters about things you once wanted to do, quoting your own words and who told you.

1. Tonight’s letters

At 8 pm (or when you choose), three envelopes arrive, each picked for the coming days: something for tonight, the weekend, someday. Open them like post. A small row of dots shows the evenings you opened your letters.

Thursday, 20:00

Your memory
wrote to you

Three letters tonight, about things you once wanted to do.

For this weekend
For a quiet evening
Someday (opened)
5 evenings with your letters this week

2. An opened letter

Your memory writes like a friend who kept your notes: what it is, your own words from back then (handwritten), who told you, and why now. Reply with “Plan it”, “Not now” or let it go for good.

1 of 3
Thursday evening
Dear Basem,

this weekend looks grey, so I thought of the pottery class in Neukölln. In August you wrote to me:

“Lena says the Sunday drop-in is great, try it when it’s grey outside.”

It’s on Sundays from 2 to 5 pm, 25 minutes from you. You’ve been carrying this one for nine weeks.

Yours, your memory
LOriginally Lena’s tip

3. A small check-in

Instead of a mood diary, one question when you open the app with nothing to do: how do you feel, and how much time is there? Your memory answers with one letter right away.

Saturday, 14:10

Nothing planned?
How do you feel?

How much time?
No ideas need to come out of this. If nothing fits, your memory just says so, and that’s fine too.
