# Oh, right!: the core concept

> *Oh, right!* is a place for the things you want to do and would otherwise forget. Some of them you do alone, some with your partner, some with friends. The app keeps them, shows what fits now, and helps turn them into plans.

Every feature is checked against this page: **does it help me remember, or help me plan?** If neither, it goes. The app never replaces talking to each other: people agree in person or in their chat, and the app only remembers and plans. Details: [roadmap](roadmap.md), [decisions](decisions.md), [target design](design/oh-app.html).

## 1. The memory is the source of truth

Your memory holds:

- **your ideas**: places, films, books, food, trips, events, anything;
- **ideas from others you chose to keep** (*Keep in my memory*), marked “Lena’s idea”;
- **dates to remember**: birthdays and anniversaries, a reminder two weeks before (a gift idea is optional);
- **things saved *for* someone**: a gift for Lena’s birthday, a book for Max (private);
- **your plans**, solo and shared, and afterwards **your history** (what you did, when, with whom).

Finding things is never a task: search, one row of categories, and **who it’s for** (*All · Just me · Lena · Friends*). When search finds nothing, *Ask your memory* lets you describe it.

## 2. Who it’s for

Every idea is **just for me**, or **in one or more spaces** (your partner, a friend, a group). That is the only “who” there is: picked when saving (*Who’s it for? Just me · Lena · The regulars…*), changed any time from the idea (*Who’s it for…*). An idea in a space always stays in your memory too. There is no abstract “with friends” tag: if the right group doesn’t exist yet, keep it just for you.

## 3. A space: one list, what fits now first

Every space (partner or group) has the same page:

1. **The weekly thing**, if there is one: a slim strip on other days, a big card on its day. For partners it is date night: *pick one of your ideas* for it. For groups the app asks on the day who’s in.
2. **Coming up**: plans in this space (*Open*, or *Join* when someone else started it; looking at a plan is not joining).
3. **Fits now**, under a season line (“🌧️ Rainy October · 8 things fit”): the best one as **today’s pick**, a big card with its note and who added it (*Plan it*, or *Another* for the next one that fits; nothing is turned down), then the rest with their reason and **Plan**. A *Last time: kayak on the Spree, 3 weeks ago* line sits above.
4. **Who’d join?** (groups only): ideas that need more people; tap *I’d join*. Once enough people would join, the idea moves to *Fits now*.
5. **Later**, folded away: out of season, a trip to plan ahead, an event far off.
6. **Add an idea**.

**What fits now** uses only what the app knows:

- **event dates**: starts or ends within three weeks, at the top;
- **season**: *warm days* / *cold days*, set when saving (optional);
- **weekends**, **how long it takes** (trips go to *Later*), **rain tonight** for cozy ideas;
- **groups**: enough people would join (2, unless the idea needs at least N).

No weather service, no location. Partners don’t vote and don’t swipe on each other’s ideas: anything in the space can be planned by either of them.

## 4. Plans

Anyone in the space picks an idea and opens the **calendar**; the app never picks a day (it marks the weekly days ♥/↻ and event days 🎟). One plan per idea: the others see it and join. **Cancel** on the calendar or the plan page puts everything back exactly as it was. A plan whose day has passed becomes a history line in every participant’s memory.

## 5. Just me and “Oh, right!” moments

*Just me* is the same list for your solo ideas (the pick card says *a note from past you*): *Fits now* first, *Later* folded away. Above it at most two *Oh, right!* lines: an occasion with a saved gift idea, your event that ends or starts soon. Rules first; AI later only for filling in from a link and finding by describing.

## 6. Fun, never homework

- No obligations, nothing nags twice, silence never blocks, there is no “no” anywhere.
- Small moments: the weekly day (“It’s on! 4 are in”), streaks, special days as emojis (🎂 14d) in the partner space.

## 7. What people see

- Tabs: **A little nudge** (Just me and the spaces), **Planned**, **My memory**.
- **The + button**: in *Just me* something to remember, a date, a plan; in a space *An idea for Lena* first, then something just for me, a date.
- **Saving** asks: what, a note, a link (optional); **who’s it for**; what kind (optional); **when is it good** (any time, warm days, cold days, weekends; optional); more details folded away (booking, cost, tags).

## 8. How we know it works

1. At least one real *Oh, right!* moment a week.
2. Ideas get added to spaces.
3. Ideas turn into plans.
4. People still use it after 30 days.

## 9. Open questions (later)

- Leaving a space: what happens to ideas you added there?
- Duplicates: two people saved the same place, merge?
- Weather: worth a weather service once seasons prove too rough?
