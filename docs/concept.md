# Oh, right!: the core concept

> *Oh, right!* is your memory of everything you want to do, and of the people you do it with. It brings the right thing back at the right moment, alone, with your partner or with friends.

Every feature is checked against this page. Details: [roadmap](roadmap.md), [decisions](decisions.md), [target design](design/oh-app.html).

## 1. The memory is the source of truth

Your memory holds:

- **your own ideas**: places, films, books, food, trips, events, anything;
- **ideas from others you chose to keep** (*Keep in my memory*), marked “Lena’s idea”;
- **dates to remember**: birthdays and anniversaries, just a reminder two weeks before (a gift idea is optional);
- **things saved *for* someone or an occasion**: a gift for Lena’s birthday, a book for Max;
- **your plans**, solo and shared;
- **your history**: what you did, when, and with whom.

Finding things must never be a task: **one row of categories** (tap *Films*, see your films), **one search box** for words and names, and a compact list. When the search finds nothing, *Ask your memory* lets you describe it (“the Japanese film about the toilet cleaner”). Nothing else on that page.

## 2. Four rules: social flows through the memory

1. **Every idea in a space comes from someone’s memory.** Nothing is created in the air; suggesting means *from my memory to Lena / the regulars*. Suggesting inside a space also saves it in your memory, marked *Suggested to Lena · waiting / a match!*, and you can edit it there. The suggest form asks what the others need: for your partner *good for date night?* and *when* (any evening, a weekend, a fixed date); for a group *does it need a minimum?* (no, whoever comes; or at least N people) and *when* (flexible, a fixed date, decide by…). Never *for whom*: gifts stay private.
2. **Keeping someone’s idea is your choice.** *Keep in my memory* puts it into your memory; saying *I’d love to* is a yes in that space, not a copy.
3. **Everything done together goes into every participant’s memory** as a history line (“Done with Lena, 12 Oct”).
4. **My memory stays yours alone and uncluttered.** Each space has its own place for what you decide together (its page, *You suggested* and *Ready to plan*).

## 3. One loop, three levels

*What shall I / we do?* → **who** (just me, Lena, the regulars) and **when** → one to three picks **from memory**, each with a reason → *Let’s do it* → a plan with smart defaults → afterwards, back into memory.

| | Just me | My partner | A group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who decides | you | a match: both say yes | a match: enough people say yes (2, unless the idea needs at least N) |
| Signature moment | the deck: a note from past you | the deck: *you both want this* → *Let’s do it* opens a calendar; emoji days; ❤️ Our story | the deck: *3 are in* → *Let’s do it* opens a calendar; “It’s on! 4 are in”, the weekly day |
| Planning | one tap | either of you; for date night, whoever picks | anyone who wants it starts the plan; the others join and edit |

**Who starts a plan:** anyone who wants the idea. There is one plan per idea: once someone starts it, the others see *Mira started a plan* and *Join the plan*, and everyone who joined can edit it. An idea is ready to plan once enough people want it; anyone who hasn’t answered still gets the card in their deck (“Aylin started a plan”), and *I’d love to* then means joining. Nobody has to wait for anybody.

**The partner space is special** (*My partner* is its own space type; a best friend is a group of two): an optional date night (*Pick one* from what you both want, or *Surprise me*) on a fixed day or flexible (taking turns if you like), *Our story* (what you did together, “one year ago today…”) and *For Lena*, a private place for her dates and, if you like, gift ideas, brought back before her birthday or your anniversary. Groups have the same model; their extra is the weekly thing, because their problem is coordinating (who’s free, who organises), not choosing.

## 4. Smart: “Oh, right!” moments

Rules first, AI later. The app brings something back when it fits:

- **occasions**: “Lena’s birthday is in 2 weeks. You saved a gift idea.”
- **deadlines**: “The exhibition ends on Sunday.”
- **context**: rain tonight, a free weekend, how long it takes, what it costs;
- **people**: with Lena, her ideas and things saved for her come first;
- **waiting**: “You’ve wanted this for 7 months.”

Later with AI: find by describing, fill in from a link, better order.

## 5. Fun, never homework

- **Moments**: the deck, the match, *Surprise me*, “Back again: Lena still loves this”, “It’s on!”, streaks.
- **Rules**: no obligations; nothing nags twice; silence never blocks; one keen person can always move things forward; *Another time* is never a hard no.

## 6. What people see

- **My memory**, **A little nudge**, **Planned**, and **spaces** (*my partner* or *a group*, up to 8).
- **The + button** opens *Add…*: in *Just me* something to remember, a date, a plan; in a space *Suggest to Lena* first, then something just for me and a date. Gift ideas come from *For Lena* or the *For someone?* question.
- **A space page is one deck.** It mixes new ideas to answer (*I’d love to / Another time*) with things you all want that fit now (tape *you both want this* / *3 are in*, a reason like “Rainy tonight: a cozy one”, and **Let’s do it**, which opens a small calendar where you pick the day (or a from–to range for trips); it only marks your date night ♥ and the event’s day 🎟, and never picks a day for you; swipe right does the same). Above it: the next plan as one slim line (or *Mira started… Join*). Below it: *You suggested 2 · see how it’s going*.
- **The partner space** is simple: special days as emojis at the top (🎂 14d, 💍 52d), 🎁 *For Lena* and ❤️ *Our story* (a pop-up, one moment at a time); then, if you have one, a highlighted **date night** card (what’s planned, or *Plan date night*: choose from *You both want* or from *My memory*; an idea from your memory is then suggested in the space too); then **From Lena**, her suggestions as a deck, and **Suggest something to Lena**; then **Your shared space**, everything you both said yes to (planned ones first with their date), each with **Plan**, now or whenever you like. No themes, no jar.
- **A group page works the same way:** the weekly things on top (up to three per space): on other days one slim strip for the next one (with *+1* if there are more; tap it to see, change, stop or add) (day and time badge, title, “in 5 days”, a little week track; orange the day before), on its day the big highlighted card, **New from the group** as a deck, **Suggest something**, then **Ready together**: plans to join (*Join*), planned ones, and ideas enough of you want (*Plan*, with who’s in).
- **Looking is not joining:** tapping someone’s plan opens it to read (*Mira’s plan · You’re looking, not in yet*, with a *Join* button); plan rows show when, where and who’s in; the Planned tab also lists *In your groups · not joined*.
- **Every plan can be cancelled:** the calendar has *Cancel*, and the plan page has *Cancel* and *Done*; *Cancel* puts everything back exactly as it was (no plan, no date night change, no RSVP).
- **Regular things are set up in their space**: date night from its card, the weekly activity from the group page; both also show under *Planned → Every week*.
- **Actions**: Suggest to…, *I’d love to* / *Another time*, *Keep in my memory*, the match (*You both want* for your partner, *Ready to plan* for groups), Plan together.
- **Regular things**: a weekly fixed activity for groups and an optional date night for partners.

## 7. How we know it works

1. At least one real *Oh, right!* moment a week for you.
2. Ideas get suggested to spaces.
3. Matches turn into plans.
4. People still use it after 30 days.

## 8. Open questions (later)

- Leaving a space: do you keep the matches you had there?
- Duplicates: if two people saved the same place, merge the cards?
- Open slots (a group’s regular afternoon to fill): left out of the MVP; bring back if testers miss them.
