# Design explorations

Earlier versions of the prototype, kept as a reference. They are not instructions: the target is [oh-app.html](../oh-app.html) and the [concept](../../concept.md). Each row says what the version had and why we moved on, so ideas we dropped don't come back by accident.

| # | File | When | What it had | Why we moved on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | [01-original-reference.html](01-original-reference.html) (and its `-fragment` source) | 2026-10-14 | The owner’s first full design of *oh! your little memory*: the look, the voice, the paper notes. | It is the origin of the look; everything later builds on it. |
| 02 | [02-our-memory-per-space.html](02-our-memory-per-space.html) | 2026-10-17 | *Our memory* per space, moving a memory into a space, date night as pick one of three. | Two memories confused who owns what. Now there is one memory per person and spaces only show what you add. |
| 03 | [03-ideas-tab-per-space.html](03-ideas-tab-per-space.html) | 2026-10-17 | A separate *Ideas* tab in each space, *Keep for me*, the jar of matches. | A second place to look. Your memory became the source, and the space shows its ideas on its own page. |
| 04 | [04-memory-with-filters.html](04-memory-with-filters.html) | 2026-10-17 | Space chips and filter rows in My memory, *I’d love to* copied ideas into your memory, open slots, a best friend as a couple. | My memory felt like a database form. It became search + one row of categories. |
| 05 | [05-anyone-plans-anything.html](05-anyone-plans-anything.html) | 2026-10-17 | Anyone could plan any idea, even without saying yes; new spaces half empty until someone joined; no demo tour. | Plans for things nobody wanted. Later: plan what you want (and, in groups, once enough people would join). |
| 06 | [06-memory-as-plain-list.html](06-memory-as-plain-list.html) | 2026-10-18 | My memory as a plain one-line list; the jar still there. | Too dry: the small paper notes came back. The jar was a list above a list and was removed. |
| 07 | [07-decks-matches-and-votes.html](07-decks-matches-and-votes.html) | 2026-10-18 | A swipe deck in every space, *I’d love to / Another time*, matches, *Your shared space*, date night with surprise turns, *Our story*. Themes (fridge, garden) were tried on top and dropped as cheesy. | Partners voting against each other is bad for a relationship, and people talk anyway. Now: remember and plan, *Fits now* with today’s pick, *I’d join* only in groups. |

**New explorations:** before a big design change, copy `oh-app.html` here as the next number with a name for what it had (`08-…html`) and add a row.
